Common questions

My progress is gone.

Progress is saved only on your device. If the app was uninstalled or you switched phones, it can't be moved or restored, and since there are no accounts we don't have a copy either. Sorry about that.

How do I change my ad settings?

Open Settings → Ad privacy settings in the game. You can also reset your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads.

Where do the paintings come from?

They're public-domain images from the Open Access (CC0) collections of The Metropolitan Museum of Art and the Cleveland Museum of Art, plus public-domain files on Wikimedia Commons. Each work's source is listed under Settings → Art credits in the game. The game isn't affiliated with these museums.

A puzzle seems wrong.

Every puzzle is checked to have exactly one solution from its clues, but if something looks off, send us the stage number and a screenshot and we'll fix it.
