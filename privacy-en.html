Privacy Policy

Logic Atelier · Effective November 27, 2026

Hankan Studio makes and runs the puzzle game Logic Atelier (Android app and web version). This page explains what information is involved when you play and where it goes.

1. We don't collect anything ourselves

There are no accounts or logins, and the game never asks for your name, phone number or email. Your puzzle progress, stars, coins, museum decorations and sound settings are stored only on your device (or in your browser). They are never sent to us, so we can't see them.

If you uninstall the app or clear the site's data in your browser, that progress is deleted and can't be recovered.

There is one exception. If you tap “Send play record” in Settings and send it to us yourself, we receive the short summary shown on screen (counts such as how often you opened the game and how many puzzles you started, finished or failed). It contains no name or device information, and nothing is sent unless you tap it. If you send it by email we will see your email address; we use it only to improve the game and delete it once we have replied.

2. Information Google receives for ads

The Android app shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • device identifiers such as the advertising ID
  • device model, OS version, language, and approximate location based on IP address
  • whether an ad was shown or tapped, and error reports

Google handles this information under the Google Privacy Policy. See also How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads. In regions where consent is required (such as the EEA and UK), the game asks for consent on first launch, and you can change your choice later in Settings → Ad privacy settings inside the game.

3. Payments

The Android app offers a one-time "Remove ads" purchase. Google Play handles the payment, and we never receive your card or billing details. The app only stores on your phone whether you bought it. Coins can only be earned by playing and can't be bought or exchanged for money.

Notifications from the app (for example, when your canvases are full) are scheduled on your phone and don't go through our servers. You can turn them off in the game settings.

4. Web version

The server hosting the web version may keep standard access logs (IP address, browser type, time of visit) for security and maintenance. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so Google may receive your IP address.

5. Children

The game isn't designed for children under 13 (under 14 in Korea), and we don't knowingly collect children's personal information. Parents or guardians can contact us below.

6. Retention and deletion

Since we don't keep any player data on our servers, there's nothing for us to store or delete. Ad-related data processed by Google is kept according to Google's own policies.
